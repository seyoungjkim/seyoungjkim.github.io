I'm Seyoung Kim, a comedy writer and performer based in Brooklyn, NY.

## Comedy
I write and perform sketch comedy with [Queer Window](https://www.instagram.com/queerwindow) at venues such as the Upright Citizen's Brigade, Caveat, Brooklyn Comedy Collective, and more. Previous teams include Magnet Theater house teams [Milwaukee!](https://www.youtube.com/@milwaukeesketchcomedy) and [Party Trick](https://www.youtube.com/@PartyTrickSketchComedy). My work has been featured in SF Sketchfest, Austin Sketch Fest, DC Sketchfest, Philly Sketchfest, and PackFest. I've also directed sketch for Magnet Theater's REMIX Diversity Sketch Lab.

I co-host and produce the monthly show [Sketch Cage Match](https://www.instagram.com/sketchcagematch/) at UCB.

I perform improv with [The Slaysians](https://www.instagram.com/theslaysiansnyc). Previous teams include OOF! and Paid 2 Be Here. I've performed at the Del Close Marathon and Fun and Dumb Improv Festival.

I created [E•MO•TION: The 10th Anniversary Comedy Variety Show](https://www.brooklyncc.com/show-schedule/emotion-10-16) and [A Sketch Show... Or Else](https://www.brooklyncc.com/show-schedule/sketchshoworelse-8-1) (with Jacob Stone).

## Film and Video
I make digital sketches with my group [sad sacks](https://www.instagram.com/sad__sacks/) and on my own.

I co-produced the webseries [Hurry Up & Grieve](https://www.instagram.com/hurryupandgrieve/) (in post-production).

I'm on the programming committee for the [Asian American International Film Festival](https://www.aaiff.org).
