I'm Seyoung Kim, a comedy writer and performer based in Brooklyn, NY.

## Comedy
I write and perform sketch comedy with [Queer Window](https://www.instagram.com/queerwindow) at venues such as the Upright Citizen's Brigade, Caveat, Brooklyn Comedy Collective, and more. Previous teams include [Milwaukee!](https://www.youtube.com/@milwaukeesketchcomedy) and [Party Trick](https://www.youtube.com/@PartyTrickSketchComedy) at the Magnet Theater. My work has been performed at festivals including SF Sketchfest, Austin Sketch Fest, DC Sketchfest, Philly Sketchfest, and PackFest.

I co-host and produce the monthly show [Sketch Cage Match](https://www.instagram.com/sketchcagematch/) at UCB.

I perform improv with [The Slaysians](https://www.instagram.com/theslaysiansnyc). Previous teams include OOF! and Paid 2 Be Here. I've performed at the Del Close Marathon and Fun and Dumb Improv Festival.

If that all sounds nice, but you'd much prefer to watch comedy from home, I make digital sketches with my group [sad sacks](https://www.instagram.com/sad__sacks/).

## Other Stuff
I helped produce the webseries [Hurry Up & Grieve](https://www.instagram.com/hurryupandgrieve/) (currently in post-production).

I created [E•MO•TION: The 10th Anniversary Comedy Variety Show](https://www.brooklyncc.com/show-schedule/emotion-10-16).

I co-wrote, produced, and directed ["A Sketch Show... Or Else"](https://www.brooklyncc.com/show-schedule/sketchshoworelse-8-1) with Jacob Stone.

I was a director for the Magnet Theater's [REMIX Diversity Sketch Lab](https://magnettheater.com/2024/08/01/remix-the-diversity-sketch-lab-2024).

I'm on the programming committee for the [Asian American International Film Festival](https://www.aaiff.org).

I used to moderate a Facebook meme group.

I play cello. I'm learning guitar.

I have 5+ years of experience as a software engineer.
