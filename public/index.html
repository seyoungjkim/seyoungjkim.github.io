Seyoung Kim

I’m Seyoung Kim: a comedian, writer, and director based in Brooklyn, NY.

Comedy

I write and perform sketch and improv comedy with Queer Window, The Slaysians, and ELLIOTT SMECK at NYC venues including the Upright Citizen’s Brigade, Caveat, Littlefield, Brooklyn Comedy Collective, and the Lincoln Center one time. Previously, I was on Magnet Theater house sketch teams Milwaukee! and Party Trick. I’ve performed at San Francisco Sketchfest, DC Sketchfest, Philly Sketchfest, Del Close Marathon, and Fun and Dumb Improv Festival.

I co-host and produce the monthly competition show Sketch Cage Match at UCB NY.

I created E•MO•TION: The 10th Anniversary Comedy Variety Show and A Sketch Show… Or Else (with Jacob Stone).

Film and Video

My latest comedy video, “The Citizen Sociologist”, was part of the 7th Tootsie’s Video Vault.

Other projects: making digital sketches with sad sacks and on my own, co-producing the webseries Hurry Up & Grieve (in post-production), and programming for the Asian American International Film Festival.
