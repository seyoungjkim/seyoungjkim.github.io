Seyoung Kim

I’m Seyoung Kim, a comedy writer and performer based in Brooklyn, NY.

Comedy

I write and perform sketch comedy with Queer Window at venues such as the Upright Citizen’s Brigade, Caveat, Brooklyn Comedy Collective, and more. Previous teams include Milwaukee! and Party Trick at the Magnet Theater. My work has been performed at festivals including SF Sketchfest, Austin Sketch Fest, DC Sketchfest, Philly Sketchfest, and PackFest.

I co-host and produce the monthly show Sketch Cage Match at UCB.

I perform improv with The Slaysians. Previous teams include OOF! and Paid 2 Be Here. I’ve performed at the Del Close Marathon and Fun and Dumb Improv Festival.

If that all sounds nice, but you’d much prefer to watch comedy from home, I make digital sketches with my group sad sacks.
